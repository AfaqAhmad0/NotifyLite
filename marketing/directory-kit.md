# NotifyLite Directory Submission Kit

## Tagline
The ultimate open source Windows notification replacement.

## 50-Word Description
NotifyLite is an open source desktop utility that replaces boring, rigid Windows notification banners with fully customizable, animated toast cards. It intercepts your system alerts, adds a scrollable notification history, per-app muting, custom sounds, and operates completely hidden from your Alt+Tab menu for a distraction-free experience.

## 150-Word Description
Are you tired of the default Windows notification center interrupting your workflow with rigid, uncustomizable banners? NotifyLite is the ultimate open source Windows notification manager. 

Using the native WinRT APIs, NotifyLite silently intercepts your notifications and renders them exactly how you want. Choose your colors, tweak your fonts, change the corner radius, and adjust the exact screen placement—or use the dynamic "Stick to Icon" feature. 

Never miss an important alert again with the built-in floating history widget that lets you scroll through past notifications. Boost your productivity by muting noisy apps or assigning them custom audio alerts. Best of all, NotifyLite is a tray-only app that hides its popups from the Alt+Tab task switcher, ensuring your workspace remains clean and focused. Free, open source, and built for power users.

## Feature Bullets
- Replace default Windows notification banners
- Fully customizable visuals (colors, fonts, sizes, opacity)
- Scrollable notification history widget
- Per-app muting and custom audio (.wav) support
- Dynamic on-screen positioning
- Completely hidden from the task switcher (Alt+Tab)
- Free, open source, and actively maintained
