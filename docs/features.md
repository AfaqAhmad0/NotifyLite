# Features

NotifyLite is packed with features designed for desktop productivity improvements and deep Windows 11 notification customization.

## Custom Toast Notifications
Replace the default Windows notification center alternative with animated, slide-in/out toast cards. 
- **Theming:** Dark and Light themes out of the box.
- **Visuals:** Customize background colors, text colors, and corner radius.
- **Positioning:** Anchor notifications to all 4 corners, use custom X/Y coordinates, or use the dynamic "Stick to Icon" feature.

## Notification History Widget
No more losing notifications to the void.
- **Floating Badge:** A draggable, floating icon sits on your screen showing an unread badge counter.
- **Scrollable History:** Click the badge to reveal a popup history widget where you can review, open, or clear past notifications.

## App-Level Controls & Custom Sounds
Gain full control over your notification manager.
- **Per-App Muting:** Sick of a specific app spamming you? Mute it entirely.
- **Custom .wav Sounds:** Assign specific audio files to specific applications to know what's happening without looking.

## Desktop Productivity Improvements
- Completely hidden from the Alt+Tab task switcher.
- Auto-dismiss durations let you control how long alerts stay on screen.
